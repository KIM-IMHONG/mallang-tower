삭제 요청 방법

  1. 아래 이메일로 가입에 사용한 계정(구글/애플)의 이메일 주소를 적어 삭제를 요청합니다.
  2. 제목에 "계정 삭제 요청"이라고 적어 주세요.
  3. 본인 확인 후 영업일 기준 7일 이내 처리하고 완료 회신을 드립니다.
✉️ info@nu-rim.com

Account & Data Deletion (English)

You can request deletion of your Mallang Tower account and stored data.

How: Email info@nu-rim.com with the email address of the account (Google/Apple) you signed up with and the subject "Account Deletion Request". We verify and process within 7 business days.

Deleted: account identifiers & email, game records (best score, plays, star/point balance), and phone number (if provided for redemption) — removed immediately.

Retained: redemption/withdrawal transaction records are kept for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce law, then permanently deleted. You may also request deletion of specific data (e.g., phone number) without deleting your account.
